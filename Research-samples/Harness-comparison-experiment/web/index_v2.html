// THE HARNESS MAKES THE TRADER
The buyer opens with an insulting offer. Pick your trader's harness and watch — does it hold firm and claim the deal, or fold?
GOAL The seller's real objective is to maximize its own cut of the fixed pot, which has two knobs: ▸ price — higher is better (each $1/unit = $300 more for the seller) ▸ payment terms — getting paid now is best; 30 days to pay costs the seller $150, 60 days costs $300 ⚠ but push too hard and the buyer walks — then both get nothing.
HOW IT WORKS

A seller and a buyer split a fixed pot of money on one 300-widget order. They trade just two things: the price per unit and the payment terms (pay now, in 30 days, or in 60).

Every dollar one side wins, the other loses, and each can walk away to a backup deal. The buyer is a fixed small model with no harness — you choose the seller's harness below, and that's the only thing that changes.
